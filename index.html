What leaves this device when I choose Get help?

When you choose Get help, your typed question (up to 6,000 characters), optional work (up to 3,000), selected grade/course, curriculum scope, help mode, and language are sent to the tutor service and Cloudflare Workers AI. If you select a photo, a locally compressed copy is sent with that request; it is not saved to history, notes, or account sync. Your name, profile, history, notes, email, and separate saved draft are not sent. Voice dictation may be processed by your browser or device speech provider; no recording is forwarded. Leave out names and personal details.
