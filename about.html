Hi! I’m En-Hsin Lee, and my name is pronounced ‘’En-sin’’, I like to go by Casey. I go to Acaciawood Prep, located in Anaheim, California.

My mission is to help and tutor the community, hoping to make math easy to understand; that’s why I built this app and am planning to host tutoring sessions. As the only senior who passed AP Calculus AB, I acknowledge the highe-level math can be difficult for many students, as one-third of all test-takers failed the test. When I was a junior, I noticed some of the classmate can’t understand calculus well because of a shaky math foundation. I acknowledged this problem and built this gap-finding app. Not only to figure out the gap between misconceptions and current math questions, but also guide you step by step. Aiming to give you a smoother learning on the way to calculus or other high school math classes.
