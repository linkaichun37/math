About the founder

Built from a problem I saw in my own classroom.

I’m En-Hsin “Casey” Lee (pronounced “En-sin”), a student at Acaciawood Prep in Anaheim, California.

While studying AP Calculus AB, I noticed that a difficult calculus problem often was not the real problem. A classmate might understand derivatives but get stuck because of an earlier gap in algebra, function notation, trigonometry, or limits. Ordinary answer generators could finish the exercise, but they did not explain where the student’s understanding first broke down.

I created Math Gap Finder to explore a different approach: read a student’s work, solve the problem independently, identify the first missing or incorrect step, and teach the prerequisite before revealing the final answer. My goal is not to replace teachers. It is to give students a patient first explanation and help them ask better questions when they meet with a teacher or tutor.

This is an evolving student-built project. AI can misread handwriting or make mathematical errors, so the app reports reading confidence, lists verification checks, and reminds users to confirm important results. I am seeking feedback from students and educators to measure accuracy and improve the tutoring approach.

Math Gap Finder is here to help you learn — never to judge.
